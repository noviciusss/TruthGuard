# Circular AH/2024/01 — Member Administration

This document is effective from 2024-02-01.

## Renewals
A renewal may be completed within 30 days after the due date.

## Operational rules

- **renewal grace period** (all active plans): 30 days after renewal due date.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
