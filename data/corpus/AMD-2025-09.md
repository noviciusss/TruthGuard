# Amendment AH/2025/09 — Network Hospitals

## Section 6
Cashless treatment requires an active network hospital and approval.

## Operational rules

- **network hospital cashless eligibility** (planned treatment at an Aurelia network hospital): Cashless treatment is available when the hospital is in the active Aurelia network and approval is obtained.

Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
