# Cashless Hospital Operations Manual

This document is effective from 2025-06-15.

## Operations
Planned admissions require pre-authorization. Emergency admissions may be notified within 24 hours.

## Operational rules

- **pre-authorization** (planned and emergency admissions): Emergency admissions may use cashless treatment without prior approval, with notification within 24 hours.
- **network hospital cashless eligibility** (planned treatment at an Aurelia network hospital): Cashless treatment is available when the hospital is in the active Aurelia network and approval is obtained.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
