# Circular AH/2025/10 — Renewal Operations

This document is effective from 2025-10-01.

## Renewals
The 30-day renewal grace period applies to all active plans.

## Operational rules

- **renewal grace period** (all active plans): 30 days after renewal due date.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
