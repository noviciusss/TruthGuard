# Aurelia Health FAQ — Network and Digital Care

## Answer
Network cashless care requires approval; members have four telemedicine consultations per year.

## Operational rules

- **network hospital cashless eligibility** (planned treatment at an Aurelia network hospital): Cashless treatment is available when the hospital is in the active Aurelia network and approval is obtained.
- **telemedicine consultation benefit** (active members): Four telemedicine consultations per policy year.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
