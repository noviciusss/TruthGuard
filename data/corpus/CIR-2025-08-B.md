# Circular AH/2025/08-B — Annual Limit

This document is effective from 2025-08-01.

## Annual limit
The Aurelia Standard plan annual hospitalization limit is 600000 currency units.

## Operational rules

- **annual hospitalization limit** (Aurelia Standard plan): 600000 currency units per policy year.
