# Amendment AH/2025/05 — Pre-existing Conditions

This document is effective from 2025-05-01.
This document amends Section 3.2 of POL-2024-V1.

## Section 3.2
The lookback period for a pre-existing condition is 48 months.

## Operational rules

- **pre-existing condition lookback** (conditions diagnosed or treated before policy start): 48 months.

Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
