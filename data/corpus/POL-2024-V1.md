# Aurelia Health Standard Policy Wording v1

## Eligibility
The Standard plan covers eligible members and covered hospitalization.

## Benefits
The benefit schedule applies to covered services.

## Operational rules

- **hospitalization waiting period** (standard plan, non-accidental hospitalization): 30 days.
- **co-payment** (plan and age dependent): 10% for standard plan members.
- **claim submission deadline** (reimbursement claims): 30 days after discharge.
- **pre-existing condition lookback** (conditions diagnosed or treated before policy start): 36 months.
- **pre-authorization** (planned and emergency admissions): All planned admissions require pre-authorization.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
