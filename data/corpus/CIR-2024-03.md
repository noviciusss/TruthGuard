# Circular AH/2024/03 — Room Rent Schedule

This document is effective from 2024-04-01.

## Schedule
Standard room rent is 1% and Premium room rent is 2% of sum insured per day.

## Operational rules

- **room rent sub-limit** (plan tier): 2% of sum insured per day for premium plan.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
