# Circular AH/2026/02 — Ambulance Claims

## Ambulance
Road ambulance reimbursement is limited to 10000 currency units per hospitalization.

## Operational rules

- **road ambulance benefit** (covered hospitalization): Up to 10000 currency units per hospitalization.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
