# Circular AH/2025/08-A — Annual Limit

## Annual limit
The Aurelia Standard plan annual hospitalization limit is 500000 currency units.

## Operational rules

- **annual hospitalization limit** (Aurelia Standard plan): 500000 currency units per policy year.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
