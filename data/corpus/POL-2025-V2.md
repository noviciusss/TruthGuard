# Aurelia Health Standard Policy Wording v2

## Eligibility
The Standard plan covers eligible members and covered hospitalization.

## Benefits
The revised benefit schedule applies from the effective date.

## Operational rules

- **hospitalization waiting period** (standard plan, non-accidental hospitalization): 45 days.
- **co-payment** (plan and age dependent): 10% for standard plan members.
- **claim submission deadline** (reimbursement claims): 60 days after discharge.
- **pre-existing condition lookback** (conditions diagnosed or treated before policy start): 48 months.
- **pre-authorization** (planned and emergency admissions): Emergency admissions may use cashless treatment without prior approval, with notification within 24 hours.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
