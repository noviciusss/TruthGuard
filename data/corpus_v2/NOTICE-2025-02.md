# Regulatory Notice AH/2025/02 — Emergency Cashless

This document is effective from 2025-06-05.

## Emergency care
Emergency admissions can proceed without prior approval, followed by notification within 24 hours.

## Operational rules

- **pre-authorization** (planned and emergency admissions): Emergency admissions may use cashless treatment without prior approval, with notification within 24 hours.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
