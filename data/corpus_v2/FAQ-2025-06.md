# Aurelia Health FAQ — Current Benefits

This document is effective from 2025-06-15.

## Current answer
The current waiting period is 45 days; the pre-existing condition lookback is 48 months.

## Operational rules

- **hospitalization waiting period** (standard plan, non-accidental hospitalization): 45 days.
- **pre-existing condition lookback** (conditions diagnosed or treated before policy start): 48 months.
- **maternity waiting period** (maternity benefit on eligible plans): 18 months for Premium plan.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
