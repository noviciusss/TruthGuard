# Circular AH/2024/02 — Co-payment Examples

This document is effective from 2024-03-01.

## Examples
Standard plan members pay 10%; members aged 60 and above pay 20%.

## Operational rules

- **co-payment** (plan and age dependent): 10% for standard plan members; 20% for members aged 60 and above.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
