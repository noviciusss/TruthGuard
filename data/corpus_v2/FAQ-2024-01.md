# Aurelia Health FAQ — Waiting Periods

This document is effective from 2024-02-15.

## Question
How long is the hospitalization waiting period?

## Answer
The Standard plan has a 30-day waiting period for non-accidental hospitalization.

## Operational rules

- **hospitalization waiting period** (standard plan, non-accidental hospitalization): 30 days.

Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
