# Circular AH/2025/06 — Emergency Admission Exception

This document is effective from 2025-06-01.
This document amends Section 5.4 of POL-2025-V2.

## Emergency exception
Emergency admissions may use cashless treatment without prior approval if Aurelia is notified within 24 hours.

## Operational rules

- **pre-authorization** (planned and emergency admissions): Emergency admissions may use cashless treatment without prior approval, with notification within 24 hours.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
