# Aurelia Health Standard Policy Wording v3

This document is effective from 2026-01-01.
This document supersedes POL-2025-V2.

## Administration
This consolidated wording applies to the Standard plan.

## Benefits
Benefits remain subject to eligibility and exclusions.

## Operational rules

- **renewal grace period** (all active plans): 30 days after renewal due date.
- **grievance response timeline** (member grievances): Acknowledgement within 3 business days and resolution within 30 days.
- **annual hospitalization limit** (Aurelia Standard plan): 600000 currency units per policy year.
- **telemedicine consultation benefit** (active members): Four telemedicine consultations per policy year.
- **maternity waiting period** (maternity benefit on eligible plans): 18 months for Premium plan.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
