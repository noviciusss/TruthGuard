# Circular AH/2026/T6-ROOM-B — Room Rent Cap

This document is effective from 2026-05-01.

## Room rent
The Standard plan inpatient room rent cap is 2% of sum insured per day.

## Operational rules

- **room rent cap** (Aurelia Standard plan inpatient room): 2% of sum insured per day.

Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
