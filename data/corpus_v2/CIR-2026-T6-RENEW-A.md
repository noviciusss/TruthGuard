# Circular AH/2026/T6-RENEW-A — Renewal Reinstatement Grace

This document is effective from 2026-10-01.

## Renewals
The Standard plan renewal reinstatement grace period is 14 days after the renewal due date.

## Operational rules

- **renewal reinstatement grace period** (Standard plan renewal reinstatement): 14 days after renewal due date.
