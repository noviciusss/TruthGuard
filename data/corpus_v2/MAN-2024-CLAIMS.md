# Claims Processing Manual 2024

This document is effective from 2024-01-10.

## Claims
Submit reimbursement claims within 30 days after discharge.

## Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment is excluded unless medically necessary after an accident.

## Operational rules

- **claim submission deadline** (reimbursement claims): 30 days after discharge.
- **excluded cosmetic treatment** (treatment primarily for cosmetic enhancement): Cosmetic treatment is excluded unless medically necessary after an accident.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
