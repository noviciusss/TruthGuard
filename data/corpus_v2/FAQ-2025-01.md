# Aurelia Health FAQ — Plan Comparisons

This document is effective from 2025-01-15.

## Question
Do plan rules differ?

## Answer
Standard and Premium plans have different room-rent limits; age can also change co-payment.

## Operational rules

- **room rent sub-limit** (plan tier): 1% of sum insured per day for standard plan; 2% of sum insured per day for premium plan.
- **co-payment** (plan and age dependent): 10% for standard plan members; 20% for members aged 60 and above.

## Contact
Members may contact Aurelia Health support for clarification.
